They already have a bat they love and a glove they finally broke in. So what do you buy a baseball kid who seems to have everything?



Think about the last time they played. Was the tape peeling off their bat? Did they ask to keep hitting after practice? Did they spend the ride home talking about their favorite MLB team? Each of those clues points to a different gift.





##### 1. Fresh bat grip tape for the kid who uses the same bat every day

If the wrap is peeling near the knob or worn smooth where they hold it, a fresh grip is a small gift they’ll use every time they swing. The Lizard Skins DSP Ultra Outburst is one option to check.



Ask how the current handle feels before picking a thickness. If they like it as is, try to match the old wrap. If they think it feels bulky, look at the thinner options. ##### 2. A hitting net for the kid who always asks for ten more swings

If they already have a batting tee and enough room to swing, a net gives them a place to hit without chasing balls after every round. The Marucci 5-foot Instant Net is a compact option to compare.



Before ordering, measure where it’ll go. Check the space behind the net and have your player slowly swing their bat through the area to check clearance. If the family already has a net or a cage they use regularly, ask whether an adjustable tee would fill a bigger gap. Which one would I buy?

For a surprise gift, I’d pick the adjustable cap if I knew their favorite team. For a kid who hits at home every week, I’d check with their parent about space and go with the net. And if I could see the tape coming off their favorite bat, I’d buy the grip and help them put it on.
