### You know their favorite team. You’ve found a hat they’d wear. Then the product page asks you to choose between 7¼ and 7⅜.



If you’re trying to keep the gift a surprise, start with an adjustable cap in their favorite team’s colors. From there, use the hats they already wear as your guide. A soft cap with a curved brim, a structured snapback, and a winter beanie each make sense for a different person.

### What if they only wear fitted hats?

Check the size printed inside a fitted cap they already wear and use that as your starting point. If you can also see the brand and model, write those down. A size from their favorite hat is more helpful when you’re shopping for the same model. If you can’t check, an adjustable hat leaves them room to dial in the fit.
