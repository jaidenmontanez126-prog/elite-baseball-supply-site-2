Your kid likes their bat, but the grip is peeling near the knob or twisting when they swing. If the bat itself is in good shape and still fits your player, a fresh wrap can get it back into the bag for about $22.



###### What thickness should you buy?

Start with how the old handle felt. If your player liked it before the tape wore out, check the bat’s original specs and try to match the thickness. If they always complained the handle felt bulky, a thinner grip is worth trying.

\- 0.5 mm adds less bulk and gives the handle a thinner feel.

\- 1.1 mm is the middle option I’d try if your player wants some padding and has no strong preference.

\- 1.8 mm gives the most padding of these three options and makes the handle feel fuller. If your player already struggles to wrap their fingers comfortably around the handle, start thinner. ##### How to rewrap the handle

1. Mark where the old grip ends. Take a quick photo so you know how far up the handle to go.

2. Peel off the old tape. Remove leftover adhesive according to the bat maker’s care instructions, then make sure the handle is clean and dry.

3. Start at the knob. Follow the new grip’s instructions for wrap direction and overlap. Peel the backing a little at a time so you can keep the wrap smooth and even.

4. Secure the top edge. Use the finishing tape, then have your player take a few easy practice swings and check whether the grip moves or bunches. [Lizard Skins’ wrapping instructions](https://www.lizardskins.com/newsroom/how-to-wrap-bat-with-lizard-skins-bat-grip)
