If you’re shopping for a youth baseball bat and see **-10** printed on the barrel, that number is the bat’s *drop*. Subtract 10 from the length in inches to get the listed weight in ounces.

| Bat length | Listed weight for a drop 10 |
|---|---:|
| 28 inches | 18 ounces |
| 29 inches | 19 ounces |
| 30 inches | 20 ounces |

## What should you check before buying?

**Check the certification stamp first.** A USA Baseball bat and a USSSA bat can both be drop 10. Ask your coach which stamp your player’s division accepts before you order.

**Then check how the bat swings.** A 28-inch drop 10 and a 30-inch drop 10 have different listed weights. If your player can try the size you’re considering, watch whether they can swing it comfortably and make consistent contact over several reps.
