If your player keeps tugging at their batting gloves between swings, check the fit before buying another pair. The fingers should reach close to the ends, the palm should lie flat around the handle, and the wrist strap should keep the glove from shifting. The easiest way to check all three is to have your player grip their own bat.

Three quick fit checks

1. Look at the fingertips. With their hand open, check for loose material past the fingertips. Then have them make a fist. If the seams press into their fingertips or the glove pulls across their knuckles, try a larger size.

2. Check the palm on the bat. Have your player grip the handle normally. If material folds between their palm and the handle, try a smaller size or a different model. If the glove feels tight as they close their hand, try a larger size.

3. Fasten the wrist strap. Have your player loosen and reset their grip a few times. The glove should stay in place without needing to cinch the strap as far as it goes.
