Your player wants to work on ground balls, but nobody’s free to hit or throw to them. With the SKLZ Rebound Fielding Trainer, they can throw a ball into the net, field the return, and repeat. You can adjust the net to practice ground balls, line drives, or fly balls.



I’d buy it for a player who already plays catch comfortably and wants to practice fielding on their own. The adjustable net earns its keep when they’ll use it for more than one drill.

What can you practice with it?

\- Ground balls: Field the ball after it bounces, bring it in, and transfer it from glove to throwing hand.

\- Line drives: Start with easy throws and practice catching the return in front of your body.

\- Fly balls: Track the ball as it comes back up and move your feet to get into position for the catch.

The net gives players a way to repeat the same drill several times. That’s especially helpful when they want to focus on one part of a play, like getting the ball out of the glove cleanly after fielding a grounder.

Try this five-ball drill

Set the net for ground balls. Have your player make five easy throws, field each return, and move the ball into their throwing hand. Pause after each rep to reset their feet. Count how many clean pickups and transfers they get out of five, then repeat at the same pace and try to beat that score.

What should you check before buying?

Look at where your player will stand and how much room they’ll have to move. The fly-ball setting needs open space overhead, and missed throws need room behind and beside the net. Set it up according to the instructions and test each angle with a few easy throws before starting a drill.



Also think about where you’ll store it. If practice means hauling it out of a crowded garage every time, measure the storage spot before ordering.

Is it worth it?

Yes, if your player regularly wants to field grounders and catch fly balls between team practices. That’s when the adjustable net gives them a reason to keep using it. If they mainly want a target for a few throws, a basic rebounder may be enough.
